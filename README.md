# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores telecom customer behavior, billing patterns, subscribed services, and customer churn tendencies using exploratory data analysis (EDA) and interactive Power BI dashboards.

The objective is to uncover patterns associated with customer retention and churn and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer loyalty.

---

## Project Objectives
- Analyze customer demographics and telecom account information
- Examine billing and payment behavior
- Investigate service subscription patterns
- Explore customer churn distribution
- Identify possible factors associated with customer loss
- Generate business recommendations from insights

## Dataset Information
The dataset contains 7,043 telecom customer records with 21 structured variables covering:
- customer tenure
- monthly charges
- payment methods
- internet services
- support services
- contract type
- churn status

---

## Key Findings
- Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn.
- Customers who churn pay higher monthly charges.
- Electronic Check users exhibit the highest churn rates.
- Customers without Tech Support and Online Security are more likely to churn.
- Fiber Optic customers show elevated churn rates.
- Customers with partners and dependents are less likely to leave.
- Gender has minimal impact on churn.


## Recommendations
- Encourage long-term contracts.
- Promote Tech Support and Online Security services.
- Review Fiber Optic customer experience.
- Encourage automatic payment methods.
- Develop retention programs for high-risk customer groups.
